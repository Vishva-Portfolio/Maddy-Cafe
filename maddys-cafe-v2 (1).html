A Story Born in Stillness

Maddy's Café was founded in 2019 by Madeleine Voss — a former barista turned flavour alchemist who believed coffee should feel like a private ritual, intimate and transformative.

"I wanted a place where the coffee is as complex as the people drinking it."

Every recipe is composed with intention. We source beans from highland Ethiopia, misty Colombian slopes, and volcanic Indonesian isles, roasting them in small batches for maximum depth.

🌿

Single-Origin Sourced

Direct trade from 12 remote farms across 6 countries.

☕

Small-Batch Roasted

Precision roasting for optimal body, clarity, and aroma.

🕯️

Candlelit Atmosphere

No harsh lights. Warm ambience crafted for conversation.
